Corporate Website & Global Export Portal

Kalasam Jaikrishna Industries

Problem
A camphor and chemical manufacturer with legacy manufacturing roots dating back to 1995—and exporting goods to over 17 countries—had no authoritative digital presence to verify legitimacy and attract direct international institutional buyers.
Solution
Engineered a high-performance corporate platform featuring an interactive product catalog with comprehensive laboratory specifications, dedicated inquiry pathways for OEM and export distributors, direct WhatsApp integration, and international technical SEO.
Key features
Dynamic product specification catalog with chemical purity grades · Dedicated multi-tier export, OEM, and bulk distributor inquiry flows · Sub-second load times engineered with lightweight semantic HTML and CSS · Automated WhatsApp lead dispatch allowing direct manufacturer communication · Structured data implementation allowing international B2B buyers to verify company credentials

B2B Ordering Platform · Android APK · PWA

JKI Orders B2B Platform

Problem
Wholesale distributors placed orders through manual phone calls and disorganized WhatsApp messages. This led to frequent dispatch tracking errors, delays in sending GST invoices, and difficult reconciliation of customer payment ledgers.
Solution
Built a unified B2B digital ordering ecosystem: a friction-free customer portal, warehouse staff administrative back-office, a lightweight Android APK package, and an installable desktop Progressive Web App (PWA).
Key features
Simplified customer authentication using mobile number and secure 4-digit PIN · 1-tap repeat order functionality based on past buying history · Real-time warehouse dispatch status tracking and transport LR number logging · Automated GST invoice generation and printable statement ledgers · Installable PWA and standalone Android APK for field operators
Android APKProgressive Web AppREST APIsRelational DatabaseCloud Back-Office

Mobile-First Grocery & Supermarket Store

Aparna Stores E-Commerce

Problem
A trusted Theni supermarket operating continuously since 1965 required an online shopping experience so local families and bulk institutional buyers could order groceries, household essentials, and bulk staples without waiting in checkout lines.
Solution
Engineered a rapid mobile-first e-commerce web application featuring intuitive category browsing, live search, promotional discounts, tiered bulk-pack pricing, dynamic cart and checkout, and automated WhatsApp order dispatch.
Key features
Lightning-fast product search with instant category filters · Tiered bulk-pack discount pricing engine for volume buyers · Frictionless mobile cart, checkout, and order history tracking · Direct integration with local home delivery logistics via WhatsApp alerts · Lightweight architecture achieving 95+ mobile performance score

How much does a project cost?

Websites start from transparent custom quotes based on exact scope and feature requirements; apps and enterprise software are quoted after a free discovery session. You always receive a fixed, upfront proposal with zero hidden surprises.

How long does technical delivery take?

A standard business or corporate website takes 1–2 weeks. E-commerce portals, B2B distributor apps, and custom software typically take 3–8 weeks depending on integration complexity.

Yes. I configure the domain, DNS, global CDN edge caching (Cloudflare), HTTPS SSL certificates, and transactional email setup. You retain 100% full legal ownership of all assets.

Mobile-First Grocery & Supermarket Store

Case Study: Digitizing a Legacy Supermarket with Bulk Pricing & WhatsApp Orders

How Prajin Dezaa engineered a production-grade digital platform for Aparna Stores, delivering immediate operational efficiency and international customer inquiries.

Visit Live Website (aparnastores.shop) ↗ Discuss a similar project →

The Challenge & Business Problem

A trusted Theni supermarket operating continuously since 1965 required an online shopping experience so local families and bulk institutional buyers could order groceries, household essentials, and bulk staples without waiting in checkout lines.

The Engineering Solution

Engineered a rapid mobile-first e-commerce web application featuring intuitive category browsing, live search, promotional discounts, tiered bulk-pack pricing, dynamic cart and checkout, and automated WhatsApp order dispatch.

Core Features Delivered

  • Lightning-fast product search with instant category filters
  • Tiered bulk-pack discount pricing engine for volume buyers
  • Frictionless mobile cart, checkout, and order history tracking
  • Direct integration with local home delivery logistics via WhatsApp alerts
  • Lightweight architecture achieving 95+ mobile performance score
